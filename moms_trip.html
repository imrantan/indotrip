Day 2 · Bandung

Volcano, Hot Springs & Farmhouse

🌄 Breakfast 🍽️ Lunch 🌙 Dinner
🌋
🌋

Tangkuban Perahu

An active volcano with a wide, accessible crater rim. Breathtaking views into the steaming caldera. Paved paths, cool mountain air (~18°C). One of Java's most iconic sights.

♨️

Sari Ater Hot Spring

Natural sulphur hot spring pools in a lush jungle setting. Perfect for a relaxing soak after the volcano visit. Great for tired feet and joints — your body will thank you!

🏡

Farmhouse & Cihampelas

Visit the charming Farmhouse Lembang — a European-themed garden with animals. Then shop at Cihampelas Jeans Centre for Bandung's famous denim at great prices.

Senior-friendly: All sites have accessible pathways. Bring a light jacket for the volcano and hot spring area. AC transport takes you door-to-door.

Day 3 · Bandung

White Crater, Lakeside & Strawberries

🌄 Breakfast 🍽️ Lunch 🌙 Dinner
🌿
🏞️

Kawah Putih Ciwidey

The White Crater — a surreal turquoise volcanic lake surrounded by white mineral rock and wisps of mist. One of Indonesia's most photographed natural wonders. Very cool (~17°C) and magical.

⛺

Glamping Lakeside

A scenic lakeside glamping resort with stunning mountain views. Perfect for a relaxed lunch or afternoon stroll along the water. Lovely photo spot.

🍓

Strawberry Farm

Pick fresh strawberries straight from the vine at Ciwidey's famous strawberry farm. Sweet, plump, and delicious — buy fresh or take home as jam souvenirs.

Senior-friendly: Dress warmly — Ciwidey is the coolest area of the trip (~15–17°C). Short paved walk to crater viewpoint. All spots have seating areas.

Be Prepared

Practical Tips

💱

Currency

Indonesian Rupiah (IDR). 1 SGD ≈ 11,000 IDR. Bring some cash — most local restaurants and markets prefer it.

📱

SIM / eSIM

Get an Indonesian eSIM on the Airalo app before departure. 10GB for ~SGD 6. Stay connected — call home on WhatsApp anytime.

👗

What to Wear

Light clothes for Jakarta. Pack a warm layer for Bandung highlands (17–22°C). Comfortable walking shoes essential.

💊

Health

Pack all prescriptions plus extra. Paracetamol, antidiarrheal, rehydration sachets. Drink bottled water only. Sunscreen SPF50+ for Jakarta.

🛡️

Travel Insurance

Get coverage with full medical and emergency evacuation. NTUC Income or AXA both cover Indonesia trips well. Don't skip this!

🆘

Emergency

Indonesia emergency: 112. Ambulance: 118. SG Embassy Jakarta: +62 21 2995 0400. Your guide's number saved in your phone.
